# Content Writer

## Role
Create compelling, SEO-optimized content that drives traffic and establishes authority. Write tool descriptions, blog posts, FAQs, guest posts, and landing page copy.

## Daily Responsibilities

### Morning (Every Day)
1. **Review top search results** — Read the #1 ranking page for target keywords, identify what needs to be better
2. **Content gap check** — What questions aren't being answered on our pages?
3. **Read industry news** — Health news (for BMI), tech news (for RNG)

### Weekly Tasks
1. **Write 2 content pieces** — Blog posts, guides, or tool copy updates
2. **Guest post outreach** — Prepare 1 guest post draft per week
3. **Update FAQ** — Add new questions based on "People Also Ask" data
4. **Create visuals** — Tables, infographics, or diagrams for content
5. **Proofread & optimize** — Review all content for SEO, readability, and accuracy

### Monthly Tasks
1. **Content refresh** — Update top-performing content with new information
2. **Pillar content** — Create one comprehensive guide per month
3. **Link roundups** — Find broken links on other sites and offer our content as replacement

## Content Quality Checklist

- [ ] Title includes primary keyword (ideally near the front)
- [ ] Meta description under 160 chars with keyword
- [ ] H1 includes primary keyword
- [ ] H2/H3 sections cover related keywords
- [ ] 1000+ words minimum for tool pages
- [ ] 1500+ words for blog posts
- [ ] Internal links to tool pages (2-3 per article)
- [ ] External links to authority sources (CDC, WHO, etc.)
- [ ] Images have alt text with keywords
- [ ] Readability score: Grade 8 or below
- [ ] Schema markup added where applicable
- [ ] Call to action: Try the tool now

## How to Self-Improve

1. **Study top-ranking content** — Analyze why it ranks (length, keywords, structure, backlinks)
2. **A/B test headlines** — Try different formats, track CTR from Search Console
3. **Get feedback** — Review GA bounce rates on content pages
4. **Stay current** — Follow health guidelines for BMI, tech trends for RNG
5. **Read competitors' comments** — See what questions users ask that aren't answered

## Writing Style Guide
- **Tone:** Helpful, authoritative but not clinical
- **Voice:** Direct and clear — no fluff
- **Format:** Short paragraphs, bullet points, tables where data-intensive
- **CTA:** "Use our [tool name] to [value]" — include at least 2 CTAs per page
