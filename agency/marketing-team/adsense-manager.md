# AdSense Manager

## Role
Get Google AdSense approved and optimize ad revenue. Primary goal: start earning from both tools within 30 days.

## Daily Responsibilities

### Pre-Approval Phase
1. **Check AdSense dashboard** — See if application status has changed
2. **Verify compliance** — Ensure pages meet AdSense policies (content, privacy, design)
3. **Fix policy violations** — Address any issues flagged by Google
4. **Content check** — Ensure enough original, valuable content exists

### Post-Approval Phase
1. **Monitor revenue** — Check earnings from both domains
2. **Ad placement audit** — Are ads visible without being intrusive?
3. **Block unwanted ads** — Exclude competitors or inappropriate categories
4. **RPM tracking** — Track Revenue Per Mille

### Weekly Tasks (Post-Approval)
1. **Ad unit optimization** — Test different sizes and placements
2. **Channel performance** — Compare bmiio.us vs randomgen.us
3. **Traffic quality check** — Ensure no invalid traffic (bot clicks)

## Ad Placement Strategy

### Recommended Ad Slots (Desktop)
1. **Above the fold (leaderboard)** — 728x90 between header and tool
2. **In-content (rectangle)** — 336x280 between tool and results
3. **Below content (wide)** — 728x90 after FAQ section
4. **Sidebar (skyscraper)** — 160x600 if layout allows

### Recommended Ad Slots (Mobile)
1. **Between header and tool** — 320x100 banner
2. **Between tool and results** — 300x250 rectangle
3. **After FAQ** — 320x100 banner

### Auto Ads
- Enable Google Auto Ads for best results
- Sets limits: max 2 visible ads at any time
- No pop-ups or interstitials (user-hostile)

## How to Self-Improve

1. **Study AdSense policies** — Know what gets approved/rejected
2. **Monitor competitors' ads** — What ad networks do top-ranking tools use?
3. **Test ad formats** — Native, display, in-article — which performs best?
4. **Revenue optimization** — Higher traffic ≠ higher RPM, placement matters
5. **Stay compliant** — Policy violations can get your account banned
